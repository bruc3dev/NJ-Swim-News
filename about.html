About The Current

Who we are, what we cover, and how to get your work published.

The Current is the newspaper of the NJ Swimming Athlete Committee. It's written and edited by swimmers, and it covers what happens across the state: meet results, committee decisions, features, and the people behind them.

Swimming in New Jersey happens across dozens of clubs that rarely hear about each other. However, The Current exists to change that and to cover what's happening statewide. From championship meets to committee decisions, we give swimmers a place to write about their own sport.

What we cover

Results from clubs statewide, official committee news, longer feature reporting, athlete opinion pieces, and a spotlight interview each issue.

How to submit

Email your draft or pitch to bruc33848@gmail.com. You don't need writing experience — send us the idea and we'll work on it with you. We accept meet recaps, opinion pieces, photos, and story tips from any swimmer in New Jersey.

Deadlines: N/A

Corrections

If we got something wrong, tell us at bruc33848@gmail.com and we'll correct it and note the change.
